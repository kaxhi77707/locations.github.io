Multan Cantt railway station and commuting

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 18 Oct 2026

Multan Cantt railway station is an important city transport landmark. For a traveller, convenient station access can be useful, but the specific street and transport route still matter.

What to look for

Check the station approach, expected traffic, parking and transport availability at the time you normally travel. For train times and services, consult Pakistan Railways rather than an old property advertisement.

ریلوے اسٹیشن کے قریب رہائش دیکھتے وقت اصل سفری راستہ، ٹریفک اور پارکنگ چیک کریں۔ ٹرین کے اوقات متعلقہ ادارے سے معلوم کریں۔

Before you decide

  • Test the station route
  • Check traffic and parking
  • Verify current train services separately

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
