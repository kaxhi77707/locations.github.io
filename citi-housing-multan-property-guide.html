Citi Housing Multan: a phase-by-phase guide

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 09 Nov 2026

Citi Housing Multan has been described in property guides as having a Bosan Road phase and another phase associated with Old Shujabad Road. These are separate locations and should not be treated as interchangeable.

What to look for

Ask which phase a seller means, then verify its exact entrance, current development and the plot or house against the relevant site map. Check documentation and all transfer requirements before agreeing a price.

Before you decide

  • Confirm the exact phase
  • See the property and approach road
  • Verify dues and transfer process

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
