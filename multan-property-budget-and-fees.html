Buying safely   ·   MULTAN

Multan property budget: costs beyond the asking price

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 22 Oct 2026

The asking price is only one part of a property transaction. Depending on the property and current rules, buyers may face transfer charges, taxes, legal costs, inspection costs and outstanding society dues.

What to look for

Request itemised, current figures from the relevant authority and your professional adviser. Keep a contingency for repairs or construction, and distinguish a refundable deposit from a non-refundable payment.

جائیداد کے بجٹ میں صرف قیمت نہیں بلکہ منتقلی، ممکنہ ٹیکس، قانونی جانچ اور مرمت کے اخراجات بھی شامل کریں۔ موجودہ شرح متعلقہ دفتر سے معلوم کریں۔

Before you decide

  • Request an itemised written estimate
  • Confirm who pays each charge
  • Keep a repair contingency

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
