DHA Multan: location and buyer checklist

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 12 Nov 2026

DHA Multan is a planned development associated with Bosan Road and Mattital Road. Property portals describe residential sectors and different plot sizes, so a sector name matters when comparing offers.

What to look for

Ask the authority or an authorised representative for the exact sector, plot number, possession status, outstanding charges and transfer procedure. A sector map is useful, but it is not a substitute for an on-site visit.

ڈی ایچ اے ملتان میں ہر سیکٹر کی ترقی اور دستیاب سہولتیں مختلف ہو سکتی ہیں۔ خریداری سے پہلے متعلقہ دفتر سے پلاٹ اور واجبات کی تصدیق کریں۔

Before you decide

  • Check exact sector and plot number
  • Ask for current transfer and development charges
  • Inspect access and nearby construction

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
