Bahauddin Zakariya University area: housing checklist

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 17 Oct 2026

Bahauddin Zakariya University is a recognised landmark in the Bosan Road area of Multan. Nearby housing listings should still be checked for their actual route and travel time to the relevant campus entrance.

What to look for

Students and families can compare transport, safety, water supply and the terms of a rental. A location described as near the university may still require a longer journey from a specific block.

Before you decide

  • Locate the correct campus entrance
  • Compare transport options
  • Read the rental terms

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
