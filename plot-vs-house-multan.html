Plot or built house in Multan: how to decide

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 28 Oct 2026

A plot and a completed house serve different needs. A plot may require construction planning and a longer timeline; a house allows direct inspection but can carry hidden repair costs.

What to look for

Calculate total cost rather than purchase price alone. For a plot, include design, permissions, utilities and construction contingencies; for a house, include inspection, repairs, taxes and transfer charges.

Before you decide

  • Compare full project budgets
  • Assess your construction timeline
  • Review documents before any deposit

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
