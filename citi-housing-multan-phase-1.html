Citi Housing Multan Phase 1

Citi Housing Multan Phase 1 is a residential project in Mouza Lutfabad on Bosan Road, Multan. Published project information identifies Bahauddin Zakariya University and DHA Multan among the landmarks associated with this corridor. The project is described by property sources as including residential plots and commercial areas. [1][2]

For anyone exploring property in Multan, the important first step is to distinguish Phase 1 from other phases. Property sources place Phase 2 on Old Shujabad Road; the locations and individual property details should therefore be checked separately rather than treated as the same project address. [1]

Citi Housing Multan Location

Phase 1 is associated with the Bosan Road side of Multan. The exact approach and travel time depend on the block, entrance, traffic and destination. A listing should provide enough information to identify the road and block on the current project map; a general “near BZU” description is not an exact plot location.

Before making a visit, ask the advertiser for the block, street, plot or house number and a map pin. Compare these with the official plan, and visit the property itself. For a house, check parking, construction quality and the condition of water and electricity connections; for a plot, check boundaries, access and possession.

Citi Housing Multan Plots & Houses

Published project descriptions refer to residential plots in categories including 5, 7 and 10 marla, alongside commercial property. Payment Plan & Current Price

Prices and payment terms are not fixed here because they depend on the block, plot position, possession, seller and timing. A price advertised for a file may not be comparable to a fully paid plot with possession. Ask for a written breakdown of the asking price, unpaid instalments, development charges and transfer fees.

For a current enquiry, share the property type, preferred block and budget. Do not send money or identity documents until the seller and the property records have been independently verified.

Approval & Ownership Verification

Some published property sources report MDA approval for Phase 1. Approval claims and scheme boundaries must be checked against current records from the Multan Development Authority. Scheme-level approval is not proof that an individual plot is clear, transferable or included within a particular approved boundary.

Ask the relevant office about the specific plot number, ownership, transfer restrictions, possession and any dues. For land-record or legal questions, seek advice from the appropriate authority and an independent qualified professional.
