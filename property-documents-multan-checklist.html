Buying safely   ·   MULTAN

Property ownership documents: a Multan checklist

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 26 Oct 2026

Property transactions may involve different records depending on whether the property is held through a housing authority, a registered deed or another arrangement. The correct documents depend on the transaction.

What to look for

Ask a qualified local lawyer or relevant authority to verify the seller, chain of ownership, transfer restrictions, outstanding dues and any encumbrances. Never rely on a photocopy or an agent’s assurance alone.

ملکیت کی مکمل جانچ کے لیے متعلقہ دفتر اور مستند وکیل سے مدد لیں۔ شناخت، سابقہ ریکارڈ، واجبات اور منتقلی کی شرائط کی تصدیق ضروری ہے۔

Before you decide

  • Verify identity and chain of title
  • Check encumbrances and dues
  • Confirm the process with the responsible authority

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
