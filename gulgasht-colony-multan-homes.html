Gulgasht Colony Multan: established neighbourhood living

An independent, practical guide for exploring property and everyday life in Multan.

Editorial desk•Archive date: 06 Nov 2026

Gulgasht Colony is an established Multan neighbourhood discussed in local area guides alongside Bosan Road. Its housing stock and commercial activity can differ considerably from street to street.

What to look for

For an existing home, look beyond the facade: ask about damp, roof maintenance, wiring, water supply and legal building changes. Compare traffic and parking on both weekdays and weekends.

Before you decide

  • Inspect structure and utilities
  • Check parking and street access
  • Compare title documents with the address

Property conditions and requirements can change. Check the details directly with the relevant authority, an independent professional and the owner before making a commitment.
